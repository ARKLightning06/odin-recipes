Odin Recipes

Welcome to Odin Recipes! You hang yourself from a branch of Yggdrasil for nine days, and in return I will bestow upon you the wisdom of 3 mighty baking recipes:

  1. Chocolate Chip Cookies are a timeless classic. Simple, easy, delicious, a great recipe to get started with.
  2. Buckeyes are a delectable combination of chocolate and peanut butter that will make your eyes water with joy. They're quick and easy, and extremely unhealthy but extremely satisfying.
  3. Smookies, or s'mores cookies, are exaclty what they sound like: marshmallows and cookies mooshed into one ooey, gooey, scrumptious snack.
