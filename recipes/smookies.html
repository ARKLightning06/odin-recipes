Smookies

Home

Prep Time: 10-15 minutes, Bake Time: 10-15 minutes, Total Time: roughly half an hour.

Description

S'mores are delicious. Cookies are delicious. Why not put them together!

These mouth-watering cookies are a combination of the classic camping treat of melted marshmallows with chocolate and the priceless age-old classic of lovingly homemade cookies. Fresh from the oven, like marshmallows fresh from the fire, they taste good. Really good.

Ingredients

For a single batch, use these ingredients. For half or double recipes, half or double all quantities.

  • 1 cup graham cracker crumbs
  • 1 1/4 cup flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 12 large marshmallows, cut in half
  • 2 Hershey's chocolate bars (24 chocolate pieces)

Steps

  1. Wash your hands!
  2. Take out 1 stick of butter to begin softening to room temperature.
  3. Begin preheating oven to 350 F.
  4. Beat butter and sugar together in mixer.
  5. Add egg and vanilla extract.
  6. Add baking soda, salt, graham crackers, and flour.
  7. Roll small balls of dough into a greased muffin tin, and press down with teaspoon to create small indentation on top.
  8. Bake for 6-8 minutes or until edges are golden brown.
  9. Remove from oven and gently press half marshmallow, sticky side down, onto each cookie.
  10. Preheat broiler, then place cookies in back of oven and broil for 1-2 minutes.
  11. Careful not to burn them!
  12. Once marshmallows are golden brown, remove and add chocolate square on top.
  13. Enjoy!
