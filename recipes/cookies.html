Chocolate Chip Cookies

Prep Time: 10-15 minutes, Bake Time: 12-15 minutes, Total Time: roughly half an hour.

Description

Chocolate chip cookies are a timeless classic. Simple, easy, delicious, a great recipe to get started with.

Best when fresh from the oven, these cookies will take melt in your mouth to a whole new level. Paired with a glass of milk and a warm cozy spot by the fire to recline in, these cookies are difficult to beat, not to mention easy to make!

Ingredients

For a single batch, use these ingredients. For half or double recipes, half or double all quantities.

  • 2 sticks butter, softened
  • 3/4 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cups brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 3/4 cups flour
  • 2 cups (one bag) chocolate chips
  • (Optional): substitute some or all the chocolate chips for white chocolate chips, chocolate chunks, nuts, MnMs, peanut butter chips, or any other appropriate substitute.

Supplies

Though many of these are required, such as an oven and a baking tray, others are simply convenient and can be replaced with substitutes with enough creativity/extra work.

  • Oven (preheated to 375 F)
  • Baking Tray (at least one, use multiple to speed up baking times)
  • Stand mixer (optional, but highly recommended)
  • Measuring cups and table/teaspoons
  • Oven mitts

Steps

  1. Wash your hands!
  2. Take out two sticks of butter to begin softening to room temperature.
  3. Begin preheating oven to 375 F.
  4. If butter is soft, mix in stand mixer. If it is still solid, microwave for short increments until it is softer (but not melted).
  5. Add 3/4 cups of granulated and brown sugar, mixing constantly.
  6. Add vanilla extract (as much as you want).
  7. Add two eggs, one at a time, mixing well in between.
  8. Add 1tsp baking soda and salt.
  9. Add 2 3/4 cups flour, mixing gently to avoid making a mess.
  10. Add chocolate chips or equivalent.
  11. Wash hands again, then take out baking tray and roll the dough into small balls, slightly smaller than a golf ball. The size doesn't really matter, but ensure size is consistent and dough balls are equally spaced.
  12. Bake for 6-8 minutes or until lightly browned.
  13. Repeat until all the dough is baked.
  14. Turn off the oven and clean up your mess. Then,
  15. Enjoy!
