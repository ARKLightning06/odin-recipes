Buckeyes

Prep Time: 15-20 minutes, Cool Time: 15-20 minutes, Total Time: roughly 45 minutes.

Description

Buckeyes are a simple yet spectacular treat to enjoy, so long as you aren't allergic to peanuts and have a good sweet tooth!

They can be a bit frustrating on the first try, but with practice will make a delicious sweet treat well worht the effort.

Ingredients

For a single batch, use these ingredients. For half or double recipes, half or double all quantities.

  • 1 stick butter
  • 1 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 1/4 cups powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 12 oz melted chocolate

Steps

  1. Wash your hands!
  2. Mix butter and peanut butter together in stand mixer.
  3. Add 2 tablespoons brown sugar and 1 1/4 tsp vanilla extract.
  4. Mix and add 3 1/4 cups powdered sugar.
  5. Wash hands again then roll the mixture into small spheres on a baking tray.
  6. Leave the tray to cool in refridgerator for 15-20 minutes.
  7. While buckeyes cool, melt chocolate with microwave or other source.
  8. Once buckeyes have chilled, wash hands again then use a toothpick to roll the peanut butter mixture in chocolate. Roll each ball in chocolate, covering all but a small circle at the top, then return to refridgerator to cool.
  9. Mistakes/disasters may happen with melty chocolate and fragile dough, especially if it isn't chilled enough. It still tastes good though!
  10. Let completed buckeyes chill for at least 5 minutes to preserve form of chocolate. For best results, take them out to thaw 5-10 minutes before consumption.
  11. Enjoy!
